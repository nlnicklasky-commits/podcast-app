## 1. What We Collect

We collect data in two categories: **data you provide** and **data generated by our system**.

**Data you provide directly:**

- **Knowledge base content** -- names, descriptions, and organization of your knowledge bases.
- **Podcast selections** -- URLs, titles, and show names of podcasts you add.
- **Chat messages** -- questions you ask within knowledge base conversations.
- **Search queries** -- text you enter when searching across podcast content.

**Data our system generates from your input:**

- **Transcripts** -- full text and timestamped segments produced by transcribing podcast audio.
- **Text chunks and embeddings** -- segments of transcript text and their mathematical vector representations, used for search.
- **AI-generated insights** -- summaries, topic lists, key points, and named entities extracted from transcripts.
- **Chat responses** -- AI-generated answers with source citations from your podcast library.
- **Processing logs** -- technical records of pipeline steps (downloading, transcribing, embedding).

**Data collected automatically:**

- **IP addresses** -- collected by our hosting providers (Vercel, Supabase) through standard web server logs. If we serve fonts from Google Fonts CDN, your IP address is also transmitted to Google; we are working to eliminate this by self-hosting fonts.

We do not collect names, email addresses, or payment information at this time. When we add user accounts, we will update this policy to cover authentication data.

## 3. Third-Party Data Processors

We share data with the following service providers, solely to operate PodBrain. Each processes data on our behalf and under our instructions.

| Provider | Data Shared | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OpenAI** | Transcript text (chunks), chat queries, audio files | Transcription (Whisper), embeddings (text-embedding-3-small), insight generation (GPT-4o), chat responses (GPT-4o-mini) | USA |
| **Groq** | Audio files, transcript text | Alternative audio transcription and AI processing | USA |
| **Supabase** | All application data (knowledge bases, transcripts, chunks, insights, conversations, search history, logs) | Database hosting, file storage, edge function compute | USA |
| **Vercel** | IP addresses, request metadata | Web application hosting and delivery | USA |
| **Podcast Index** | Search query strings | Podcast discovery API | USA |
| **Google Fonts** (planned for removal) | IP addresses, User-Agent, Referer headers | Font delivery | USA |

We require Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) from each processor. OpenAI's DPA is available at [openai.com/policies/data-processing-addendum](https://openai.com/policies/data-processing-addendum/).

**International transfers:** All processors are based in the United States. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as adopted by the European Commission to safeguard transfers under GDPR Articles 44-49.

We do **not** sell your personal data to any third party.

## 4. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Knowledge bases, podcasts, transcripts, chunks, insights | Retained while your account is active; deleted upon account deletion request |
| Chat conversations and messages | Retained while your account is active; deleted upon account deletion request |
| Search history | 90 days, then automatically purged |
| Processing logs | 30 days, then automatically purged |
| Audio files | Deleted immediately after transcription is complete |

When you delete a knowledge base or podcast through the app, associated transcripts, chunks, insights, and conversations are permanently deleted via cascading deletion.

## 5. Your Rights

### For all users

You may request to:

- **Access** your data -- receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Delete** your data -- request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data.
- **Export** your data -- receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format.

### Additional rights under GDPR (EEA, UK, Swiss residents)

- **Rectification** -- correct inaccurate personal data.
- **Restriction** -- limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
- **Object** -- object to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Withdraw consent** -- where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.
- **Lodge a complaint** with your local Data Protection Authority.

### Additional rights under CCPA (California residents)

- **Right to Know** -- request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected.
- **Right to Delete** -- request deletion of personal information we have collected from you.
- **Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing** -- we do not sell or share your personal information as defined by the CCPA. No opt-out is required, but you may contact us to confirm.
- **Non-Discrimination** -- we will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email **nl.nicklasky@gmail.com**. We will respond within 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA).

## 6. Security

We implement the following measures to protect your data:

- All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted via TLS (HTTPS).
- Database access is restricted through Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) policies.
- API keys for third-party services (OpenAI, Groq, Podcast Index) are stored as server-side secrets and are never exposed to the browser.
- Audio files are deleted from storage immediately after transcription completes.
- Edge Function endpoints are restricted to authorized origins via CORS policies.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a data breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you and any applicable regulatory authority as required by law.
